Transit Hub Harlem

Reimagining Montefiore Square as a Multimodal Gateway

Start

The Project Goal

Optimizing the transfer between the 137th St Subway and Broadway Bus Lines.

The Bottleneck

Current sidewalk widths cannot support the peak-hour volume of commuters transferring from rail to bus.

Safety & Sightlines

Existing hardscape lacks clear wayfinding, creating confusion and safety risks for night-time travelers.

The Redesign Proposal

My proposal introduces a glass-and-steel transit pavilion. This structure houses digital real-time arrival kiosks and provides a weather-protected zone for bus commuters, effectively separating waiting areas from pedestrian flow paths.

Reflection on AI Collaboration

The AI Contribution

DALL·E was essential for visualizing the high-tech materials and lighting of the new transit pavilion. It allowed me to iterate on how digital signage and modern glass structures would integrate with the existing Harlem streetscape.

Human Design Logic

While AI rendered the aesthetic, the site logic was human-driven. I identified the specific friction points at 137th street and ensured the design followed NYC DOT's focus on accessibility and commuter efficiency.
