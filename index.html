Reimagining Montefiore Square

A Multimodal Gateway for the Harlem Community

The Project Goal

The 137th St - City College station is a crucial piece of infrastructure. Our goal is to optimize the transfer between the subway and Broadway bus lines by improving sightlines and commuter flow.

Safety & Sightlines

By opening up the square and using modern glass-and-steel materials, we increase "eyes on the street" and enhance public safety for all travelers.
