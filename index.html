Transit Hub Harlem

Reimagining Montefiore Square as a Multimodal Gateway

Start

The Project Goal

Optimizing the transfer between the 137th St Subway and Broadway Bus Lines.

The Bottleneck

Current sidewalk widths cannot support the peak-hour volume of commuters transferring from rail to bus.

Safety & Sightlines

Existing hardscape lacks clear wayfinding, creating confusion and safety risks for night-time travelers.

The Redesign Proposal

My proposal introduces a glass-and-steel transit pavilion. This structure houses digital real-time arrival kiosks and provides a weather-protected zone for bus commuters.

Reflection on AI Collaboration

The AI Contribution

AI was essential for visualizing the high-tech materials and lighting of the new transit pavilion.

Human Design Logic

While AI rendered the aesthetic, the site logic was human-driven. I identified the specific friction points at 137th street.
