Reflection: Human-AI Collaboration

The AI Role

I utilized DALL·E to rapidly iterate on complex architectural forms that are difficult to code from scratch. The AI helped visualize how modern materials—like structural glass and integrated LED lighting—would look within the specific footprint of Montefiore Square. It allowed me to test "Transit Hub" concepts like digital wayfinding and modular shelters in seconds, providing a cinematic quality consistent with the Helios design language.

The Human Role

While the AI generated the visuals, the "Transit Hub" logic was entirely human-driven. I had to guide the AI to respect NYC DOT standards and specifically address the bottlenecks at the 137th St subway entrance. Human judgment was required to filter out unrealistic AI "hallucinations" and ensure the final proposal remained a functional, accessible public space centered on the needs of Harlem commuters.
